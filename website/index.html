Core Innovations

Built for Sovereign Communications

Engineered for festivals, natural emergencies, flights, remote expeditions, and privacy-conscious communities.

🎯

60 FPS Concentric Radar

Visual distance rings for <2m, ~5m, and ~10m+ calculated with Kalman-smoothed RSSI filters and deterministic angle hashing so blips never jitter.

🔐

XChaCha20-Poly1305 E2EE

Ephemeral Diffie-Hellman key exchange provides forward secrecy. Compare a 6-digit visual safety code to verify authenticity with no certificate authority.

📦

Smart Offline Queue

Messages composed while contacts are out of range are persisted in SQLite and auto-flushed the microsecond their beacon is detected on radar.

⚡

Dual-Engine Transfer

Small payloads stream over BLE GATT with CRC32 checksums. Large 4K videos and PDFs negotiate Android Wi-Fi Direct for 50+ MB/s transfers.

🛡️

ConnectedDevice Service

Android foreground service keeps scanning and advertising active continuously even when your phone is locked or tucked in your backpack.

👁️

Zero-Trace Invisible Mode

Stop advertising your beacon completely while continuing to scan and monitor the radar mesh for nearby friends.

Wire Protocol

The Serverless BLE & Wi-Fi P2P Architecture

Nearby replaces centralized internet servers with physical radio negotiation. Devices discover each other via 128-bit custom Service UUID advertisements:

  • BLE Service UUID: 0000fee0-0000-1000-8000-00805f9b34fb
  • Packet Structure: Sequence numbers, 32-bit CRC, and final SHA-256 chunk assembly
  • Wi-Fi Direct P2P: Negotiated over GATT; establishes direct socket streams on port 8988
